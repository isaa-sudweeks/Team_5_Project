# Safety Record

Our group took apart a nerf gun. The nerf gun had not electric components including capacitors, batteries, nor a plug. The nerf gun was purely mechanical. Because of this there was no risk of electric shock or fire.

In preparation for the teardown our group met in the project support center and put on personal protective equipment including safety glasses. We then pressed the trigger to ensure the main load spring was not stretched. Then after ensure no energy was stored in any of the springs we removed all of the screws. We then carefully pulled the two halves apart ensuring not to break any brittle plastic pieces.
